Text 1

Emma was walking home from school when she found an old blue backpack on the dirt. It looked ordinary, but when she picked it up, something strange happened. The backpack felt warm and light, almost like it was alive.

At home, Emma opened the backpack and was surprised to find it was full of beautiful, colorful books. She had never seen these books before. When she touched the first book, it opened by itself and the pages began to glow softly. The story was about a brave girl who could talk to animals.

Emma read for hours without getting tired. When she finished the first book, another one appeared in the backpack. This time it was about a boy who could fly. Emma realized that this was no ordinary backpack. It was magic!

Every day after school, Emma would sit in her room and read the magical books. Each story taught her something new about courage, friendship, and kindness. Emma kept the magic backpack secret, but she became happier and more confident every day.

Text 2

Many people around the world are choosing to cycle more often, not just for sport but also as a daily way to travel. Cycling offers numerous advantages for both people and the environment.

Firstly, it's an excellent form of exercise. Regular cycling can significantly improve your heart health, strengthen your muscles, and help you manage your weight. It's a low-impact activity, which means it is gentle on your joints, making it suitable for many ages. People often feel more energetic and less stressed after a good bike ride.

Secondly, cycling is very environmentally friendly. Unlike cars or buses, bicycles produce no harmful emissions, helping to reduce air pollution in our cities. They also take up less space and contribute to less traffic congestion. For shorter journeys, cycling is often quicker than driving, especially in busy urban areas.

Finally, cycling can be very cost-effective. You don't need to pay for fuel, parking, or expensive public transport tickets every day. Its maintenance costs are generally low. Many cities are now building special bike lanes, making it safer and more convenient for cyclists. So, if you're looking for a healthier, greener, and cheaper way to travel, perhaps it's time to get on a bike!
